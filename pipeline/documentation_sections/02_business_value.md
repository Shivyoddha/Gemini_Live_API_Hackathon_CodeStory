## Business Value

The IRIS Procurement Portal provides significant business value by automating and structuring the procurement request and approval processes. It eliminates manual, paper-based workflows, reducing human error and accelerating approval cycles. Key benefits include:

*   **Enhanced Efficiency:** Automates the sequential approval process for procurement documents (Doc A and Doc B), reducing administrative overhead and processing time.
*   **Improved Transparency and Traceability:** Provides a clear audit trail for every document, capturing approval dates, remarks, and the responsible participant at each stage. Users can track the real-time status of their requests.
*   **Clear Accountability:** Defines distinct roles (Buyer, Approvers P, Q, R, S) and their responsibilities within the workflow, ensuring clear accountability for actions taken on procurement requests.
*   **Data Integrity:** Centralizes procurement data in a structured database, ensuring consistency and accuracy of equipment details, costs, and approval histories.
*   **Scalability:** The Ruby on Rails framework with PostgreSQL provides a solid foundation for future enhancements and scaling of procurement operations.
*   **Automated Document Generation:** Automatically generates Document B upon the completion of Document A, streamlining the secondary approval phase and reducing manual data entry.
*   **Proactive Communication:** Email notifications keep buyers and approvers informed of document status changes, pending actions, and rejections, facilitating timely responses.
